**SIEM engineering.** Custom DSMs for FortiEDR, FortiWeb, Splunk, Sentinel, SolarWinds and ESET. Log normalization, correlation rule tuning and false-positive reduction.

**Sentinel.** KPI and telemetry workbooks, and end-to-end client onboarding: log sources, data connectors and SIEM setup.

**Reporting.** Per-client EPS tracking with Python, SQL and Power BI.

**Blue team practice.** Home lab with Active Directory, Splunk and ELK for threat hunting, log analysis and purple-team exercises, alongside TryHackMe and Hack The Box.
